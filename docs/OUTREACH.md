# Outreach messages

Use these to book the 15–20 interviews in `docs/INTERVIEWS.md`. Keep each
message short, ask for 20 minutes, and make it about learning, not selling.
Personalise the first line every time. A generic message gets ignored.

Send about 40 messages to get 15 calls. Track every one in the interview
tracker.

## LinkedIn connection note (300 characters max)

> Hi {name}, I saw you're {working on agents at X / posted about MCP}. I'm a
> cybersecurity student researching how teams control what AI agents can do to
> files. Could I ask you 3–4 questions over 20 minutes? Not selling anything.

## LinkedIn message or email

**Subject:** 20 minutes on AI agents and file access?

> Hi {name},
>
> {One line about something specific they did: a post, a talk, their product.}
>
> I'm Cayden, a cybersecurity student building a tool that gives AI agents a
> sandboxed, reviewable copy of files instead of direct access. Before I build
> more, I'm talking to people who run agents for real to learn what actually
> goes wrong.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week or next? I'll ask about your
> experience, not pitch you. Happy to share what I learn from everyone else.
>
> Thanks,
> Cayden

## Community post (Discord, Slack, Reddit, forums)

Check each community's self-promotion rules first, and post where research
questions are welcome.

> **Researching: how do you control what AI agents can do to your files?**
>
> I'm a security student looking into how teams handle agents that read and
> write real files (docs, spreadsheets, contracts, not just code). Things like:
> what they're allowed to touch, how you see what they changed, and how you
> undo mistakes.
>
> If you've run into this, I'd love 20 minutes of your time. DM me. I'll
> share a summary of what I learn with everyone who talks to me.

## Asking a friend or professor for intros

> Hey {name}, I'm working on a startup idea around safely giving AI agents
> access to company files, and I'm interviewing people who use agents at work.
> Do you know anyone who runs agents or works in security and might give me 20
> minutes? A quick intro would help a lot. Here's a blurb you can forward:
>
> "Cayden is a cybersecurity student researching how teams control AI agents'
> access to files. He's looking for 20-minute conversations, not selling
> anything."

## Follow-up (send once, 4–5 days later)

> Hi {name}, just bumping this in case it got buried. Even 15 minutes would
> help. If now's not a good time, no worries at all.

## After the call

> Thanks again for your time today, {name}. {One specific thing you learned
> from them.} You mentioned {person}; would you be open to introducing us?
> I'll send you the summary of what I hear across all the conversations.
